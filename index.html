Personal rclone Drive Backup

This is a private, non-commercial backup utility that uses the open-source rclone tool to access Google Drive accounts explicitly authorized by their owner.

The utility runs on a server controlled by its owner. It is used for personal file backups, data migration, and verification of file integrity between storage services selected by the owner. It is not offered as a public service.

Google Drive permissions are requested through OAuth and used for file and folder operations needed by the owner, including listing, reading, creating, updating, and verifying data. Permissions can be revoked in Google Account settings.

Privacy Policy

This informational website does not ask visitors to enter Google credentials or access tokens.
